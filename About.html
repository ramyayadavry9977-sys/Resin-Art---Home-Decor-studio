FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A Few Things You May Like to Know.

Everything you need to know about our handmade creations, custom pieces, and the little details behind our work.

Do you create custom resin pieces? +

Yes! We love creating personalised pieces. You can share your idea with us, and we'll work together to create something meaningful and unique.

Is every piece handmade? +

Yes, every creation is carefully handcrafted with attention to detail. This makes each piece special and beautifully unique.

Will my handmade piece look exactly like the photos? +

Handmade art naturally has small variations in colour, texture, and patterns. These little differences are what make every piece truly one of a kind.

How can I place a custom order? +

Simply get in touch with us through our contact page and share your idea. We'll guide you through the process and discuss the details of your custom creation.

How should I care for my resin artwork? +

Keep your piece clean using a soft cloth and avoid harsh chemicals or extreme heat. With a little care, your creation can remain beautiful for years to come.
